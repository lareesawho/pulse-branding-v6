Why sectors matter

Where complexity becomes friction.

Sector experience helps us recognize recurring tensions faster: where complexity becomes friction, where trust is fragile, where category conventions create sameness and where the customer journey behaves differently.

A life-sciences buyer, an industrial procurement team and a luxury consumer do not need the same proof. The sector tells us which questions matter; the business tells us what to do about them.
