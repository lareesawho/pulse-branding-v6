02 / 06Brand identity

Make the idea
impossible to mistake.

We turn strategy into a visual and verbal system people can recognise, remember and trust, across a package, a screen, a sentence or a moving interface.

Build the identity system
Visual identityVerbal identityMotionBrand centre

The test

Remove the logo.
Is the brand still there?

Recognition comes from a system of signals: type, colour, image behaviour, language, proportion, motion and repetition. A logo can participate in that system. It cannot substitute for one.

01 · NeoLabCare

One object had to carry an entire routine.

The system became quiet, precise and reduced enough to make compression feel intentional rather than compromised.

02 · Robotist

Robotics needed a host.

The character and wordmark make a technical category feel easier to enter, without making the technology childish.

03 · Vodkiss

The occasion was part of the product.

Pack structure, label and gifting behaviour were designed together rather than decorated separately.

04 · GramLingo

The teacher was also the identity.

Character rules came first, because the mascot had to survive lessons, navigation, animation and the scale of an app icon.

How a system is built

One idea.
Seven expressions.

Position→Recognition device→Type→Colour→Imagery→Language→Motion

The sequence matters. Colour cannot rescue an unclear position. Motion cannot create recognition that does not exist when the screen is still.

Digital behaviour

A brand is not static once it enters a screen.

Interfaces reveal whether an identity is really a system. Type has to respond. Colour has to carry state. Motion has to communicate hierarchy. The brand has to survive interaction.

State

Progress is carried by the character, not by a progress bar.

Hierarchy

One decision per screen. Everything else recedes.

Continuity

The same rules hold from a 60px icon to a full interface.

What we build

Enough structure to stay coherent. Enough freedom to stay alive.

Visual identity

Mark, typography, colour, imagery and recognition rules.

Verbal identity

Proposition, message hierarchy, tone and repeatable language.

Motion behaviour

How the identity reveals, transitions and responds.

Brand centre

A practical source of truth for the people using it after launch.

Make the strategy visible.

If the business already knows what it needs to mean, we can turn that direction into something people recognise before they consciously analyse it.
