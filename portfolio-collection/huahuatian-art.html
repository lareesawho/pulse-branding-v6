Brand Creation.

Goal

Huahuatian needed a space and brand that reflected the boundless creativity of children, with a venue, visual identity, and advertising that felt fun, fresh, and full of life.

Solution

We redesigned their space to be a playful, inspiring environment. The visual identity we crafted was vibrant and full of character, while the advertising and displays were designed to captivate both kids and parents.

What we delivered.

  1. Brand Strategy

    Developed a brand strategy that focused on creativity, fun, and inspiration.

  2. Visual Identity

    Created a playful and vibrant visual identity to capture the essence of childhood imagination.

  3. Venue Transformation

    Redesigned the venue to become a creative wonderland for young artists.

  4. Advertising Campaign

    Produced engaging ads that appealed to both kids and their parents.

Where it landed
