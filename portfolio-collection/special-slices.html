Goal

To create an inclusive brand identity that educates and resonates with a global audience on special needs awareness.

Solution

We designed a bold, manga-inspired visual identity and a user-centric website to convey the series' mission of inclusion effectively.

What we delivered.

  1. Visual Identity

    Created a vibrant, mangainspired identity to convey the story of inclusion.

  2. Online Platform

    Built an accessible online platform to share the manga series widely.

  3. Storytelling Campaign

    Developed narratives to connect emotionally with diverse audiences.

  4. Community Engagement

    Organized events to involve the community and promote inclusion.
