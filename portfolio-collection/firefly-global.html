A more human path
to care.

Goal

Help adults and teenagers in California understand who provides care, what telehealth support is available and how to take the next step.

Experience

Bring the provider, care information and appointment path together in one straightforward journey, with phone and booking options easy to find.

Clear. Calm. Considered.

  1. Lead with the person

    Introduce the provider and make the practice feel approachable from the first visit.

  2. Explain care plainly

    Set clear expectations around California telehealth and available support.

  3. Make the next step clear

    Keep appointment and contact options easy to find.

Scope note

This case describes the public website experience. Patient outcomes, booking volume and clinical results are not claimed.
