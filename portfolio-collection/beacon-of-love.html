Goal

Each year, over 150,000 children in China are born with congenital heart disease, with many unable to afford necessary surgeries

Solution

Beacon of Love provides financial assistance for life-saving surgeries, ensuring these children receive the care they need.

What we delivered.

  1. Brand Strategy

    Developed a brand strategy that conveys hope and community trust.

  2. Visual Identity

    Designed a warm and caring visual identity to build emotional connections.

  3. Website Development

    Built a website that tells the stories of children helped and attracts donors.
